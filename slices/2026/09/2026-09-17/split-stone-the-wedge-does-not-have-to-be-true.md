# SPLIT STONE — THE WEDGE DOES NOT HAVE TO BE TRUE

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** CANDIDATE / FORMATION WITNESS  
**Promotion:** none by appearance here; proposed canonical home is ALEX.2

## What was noticed

A recurring Static Collective research habit finally acquired a bounded shape.

The habit is older than the name:

- give a live problem a deliberately strange, funny, paradoxical, or wrong framing;
- keep that framing low-authority;
- use it to break habitual categorization;
- inspect the fracture for relations that were difficult to see from the ordinary frame;
- discard the provocation itself as evidence;
- pressure-test whatever survives independently;
- keep the failed paths and the receipt.

Working name:

# WEDGE

Formation / stupid name:

# SPLIT STONE

Compression:

```text
ORDINARY FRAME
    ↓
WEDGE
    ↓
CANDIDATE FRACTURE
    ↓
PRESSURE
    ↓
SURVIVOR / REFUSAL
    ↓
RECEIPT
```

The candidate seal is:

> **THE WEDGE DOES NOT HAVE TO BE TRUE. THE FRACTURE HAS TO BE REAL.**

This does not mean that nonsense becomes evidence.

It means an explicitly non-authoritative perturbation may be useful for discovering which distinctions, dependencies, invariants, or alternative relations become visible when the ordinary decoder is disturbed.

## The important correction

A useful absurdity is not a supported claim.

```text
WEDGE OUTPUT != EVIDENCE
WEDGE OUTPUT != SUPPORT
WEDGE OUTPUT != ANCESTRY
WEDGE OUTPUT != AUTHORITY
```

The wedge may choose where to strike.

The resulting candidate relation must survive without leaning on the wedge that exposed it.

This is already compatible with ALEX formation discipline:

```text
DISCOVERY TRACE != EVIDENCE PATH
```

and:

> Serendipity may choose the door. Evidence decides what can walk through it.

## Beta contract

A first bounded form is eight moves.

### 1. BASELINE

State the ordinary frame or decoder clearly enough that a perturbation can be compared against it.

### 2. WEDGE

Apply one declared absurd, paradoxical, inverted, deliberately wrong, or otherwise frame-breaking move.

Examples may include:

- absurd relabeling;
- impossible hypothetical;
- deliberate category inversion;
- contradictory framing;
- controlled wrong decoder;
- surprising juxtaposition;
- comic / koan-like handle.

The wedge is marked `DISCOVERY_TRIGGER_ONLY`.

### 3. FRACTURE

Record what becomes newly distinguishable, reachable, questionable, or structurally visible.

Do not yet call it true.

### 4. DELTA

Ask what actually changed.

```text
changed decoder?
changed representation?
changed object?
changed reachable relation-space?
changed observer cut?
changed only our attention?
```

Where formalizable, Dogram may compute this delta.

### 5. HARVEST

Extract candidate relations from the fracture.

The harvest is a proposal set, not an admission set.

### 6. DROP WEDGE

Remove the original absurdity from the support path.

If the candidate only looks interesting while the joke, paradox, wrong decoder, or evocative metaphor remains load-bearing, it has not survived.

### 7. PRESSURE

Route the survivor into ordinary ALEX PRESSURE:

```text
LITERALIZE
ATTACK
NEIGHBOR SEARCH
SEPARATE BRIDGES
KILL OVERCLAIMS
SURVIVOR
PREDICT
PRESSURE AGAIN
```

The wedge must be permitted to yield nothing.

### 8. RECEIPT

Keep:

```text
BASELINE
WEDGE
FRACTURE
DELTA
HARVEST
WHAT DIED
WHAT SURVIVED
WHY IT SURVIVED
NEXT DISCRIMINATOR
```

The failed wedge remains formation history even when it contributes zero evidentiary weight.

## Historical neighbors, not genealogy

A research lookabout found several older practices with partial structural similarity.

They are preserved as **independent neighbors**, not claimed ancestors.

### Socratic aporia

Socratic questioning can drive a confident conceptual account into contradiction or aporia without immediately supplying a replacement. Modern pedagogical scholarship has compared this redirection of attention to koan practice.

Neighbor relation:

```text
confident frame -> contradiction -> conceptual opening
```

Reference: Mark E. Jonas, “Education for Epiphany: The Case of Plato's Lysis,” *Educational Theory* 65.1 (2015), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/edth.12094

### Chan / Zen koan and encounter practice

Scholarly descriptions of koan practice emphasize paradox, contradiction, non sequitur, strange action, and disruption of ordinary discursive framing. Importantly, the point is not permanent nonsense; the break is used to alter how the situation can be apprehended.

Neighbor relation:

```text
habitual frame -> paradox / shock -> break -> changed apprehension
```

References:

- Jennifer McWeeny, “Liberating Anger, Embodying Knowledge,” *Hypatia* 25.2 (2010), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1527-2001.2009.01077.x
- Steven Heine, “Ch'an Buddhist Kung-ans as Models for Interpersonal Behavior,” *Journal of Chinese Philosophy* 30.3-4 (2003), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6253.00135
- Eric S. Nelson, “Language and Emptiness in Chan Buddhism and the Early Heidegger,” *Journal of Chinese Philosophy* 37.3 (2010), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6253.2010.01597.x

These works do not establish a transmission path into Static practice.

### Defamiliarization / artistic “making strange”

The broader artistic method of making a familiar object strange enough to become perceptible again is another close functional neighbor.

Neighbor relation:

```text
automatized perception -> altered presentation -> renewed inspection
```

Again:

```text
resemblance != ancestry
```

## Candidate test

Before WEDGE deserves promotion into the ALEX skill surface, it should survive multiple materially different specimens.

At minimum:

1. **absurd relabeling specimen** — a joke-name or koan-like frame reveals a useful relation;
2. **broken-decoder specimen** — an intentionally wrong decoder reveals a dependency or invariant;
3. **category-inversion specimen** — an impossible or contradictory framing exposes a distinction missed by baseline analysis;
4. **null specimen** — a provocative wedge produces nothing useful and the method cleanly returns `NO_FRACTURE_EARNED`.

A healthy method must be able to fail.

## Epistemic split

**Documented fact:** Earlier Static work already used deliberately strange frames, controlled wrong decoders, MADDCL0WN counterpressure, FAR-SIDE traversal, and ALEX formation/evidence separation. The cited scholarly literature documents older practices involving aporia, paradox, conceptual disruption, and non-propositional redirection of attention.  
**Inference:** These pieces can be compressed into a useful candidate method in which a low-authority provocation is used to discover candidate relations before independent pressure.  
**Speculation:** WEDGE may deserve promotion into ALEX as a reusable research mode if materially different specimens show that it repeatedly discovers useful structure without increasing evidence laundering or false-positive pattern production.
