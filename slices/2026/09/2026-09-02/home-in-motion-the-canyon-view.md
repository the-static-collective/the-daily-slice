## 7. The important distinction: coexistence is not braid

If HOME and MOTION simply coexist without affecting each other, order does not matter.

That gives a product-like structure:

```text
HOME then MOTION
=
MOTION then HOME
```

Useful, but not yet NuOmetry.

The live seam appears when:

```text
HOME changes the available MOTION
and
MOTION changes the realized HOME
```

Then:

```text
HOME -> MOTION
```

can differ from:

```text
MOTION -> HOME
```

The ingredients can be identical while the crossing history is different.

That is the canyon-view version of:

> **SAME PARENT SET != SAME PARENTAL BRAID.**

## 8. The braid receipt

When the two transformations do not commute, the difference between the two orders can itself be retained as a mathematical object.

Conceptually:

```text

        |
        v

versus

        |
        v

```

If those paths land differently, the delta is not in which components were present.

It is in **how they crossed**.
