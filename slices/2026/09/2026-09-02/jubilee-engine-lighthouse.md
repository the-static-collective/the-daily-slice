## Epistemic split

**Documented fact:** For a three-element set `{P,F,C}`, there are exactly three unordered two-element subsets. In the declared triangle there are six vertex-edge incidences. Counting both orientations of each of those six incidences yields twelve directed incidence records.  

**Inference:** `Protection`, `Faithfulness`, and `Connection` may form a useful three-field relational basis; the pairwise names `FREE`, `BEAR-WITH`, and `GIVE-SELF` may be useful enacted projections; a coherent whole may be usefully addressed as `LOVE`; and a directed incidence may be a useful model for a lawful handoff.  

**Speculation:** This grammar may illuminate theological language, moral discernment, human/AI handoffs, distributed systems, narrative continuity, or other domains. None of those mappings is established merely by this formation witness.

## Refusal boundary

This slice does **not** establish:

- `Father = Protection`, `Son = Faithfulness`, or `Spirit = Connection` as doctrine;
- `LOVE = P × F × C` as a universal theorem;
- moral judgment as invalid;
- the number `12` as intrinsically significant;
- reversibility of every handoff;
- consent, authorization, or obligation from an available edge;
- that every relation in reality is literally a Y branch.

The construction must narrow, split, or die if the ports cannot reproduce its discriminators without smuggling the desired result into their decoders.
