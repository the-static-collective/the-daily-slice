# THE DOOR HAS ANCESTRY

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Status:** CANDIDATE SYNTHESIS / DURABLE SLICE  
**Promotion:** no shared-kernel promotion; preserve as a cross-stack research law and executable target

## The compression

A claim can be well supported after discovery while the route that ever made it discoverable has a completely different ancestry.

That gives at least four distinct questions:

```text
WHY REACHABLE
    what infrastructure, decoder, selector, index, aperture, capability,
    or prior interest made this material enter the actor's reachable world?

WHY NOTICED
    what was contacted, attended, decoded, revisited, or passed over?

WHY BELIEVED
    what evidence and derivation support the claim?

WHY USED
    what materially entered formation of the later output?
```

These questions may overlap in one history, but they are not interchangeable.

> **The route by which a claim entered an actor's world is causal history, not evidentiary support.**

## Why this matters now

Several independently developing surfaces have converged on the same seam:

- ALEX `INTEREST-CONSUMPTION-001` observes that an interest receipt can remain inert until a later selector actually reads it.
- ALEX `PROJECTION-INVARIANCE-001` requires hidden differences not to alter a bounded result without an attributable path.
- 3rdi's active UNDERSTORY work distinguishes exposure, contact, attention, decoder application, and stance rather than collapsing them into one observation event.
- BOOKROOM/access-engineering work distinguishes a source supporting a claim from the index, decoder, or aperture that made the source reachable.
- MORTAL-ACTOR requires every actor to reason from a world it actually inhabited.

The new object is not a master provenance graph. It is a missing **question** that existing receipts should be able to answer:

> **Why was this possibility reachable to this actor at this cut?**

## Interest does not become magical

Suppose an attributable interest receipt exists:

```text
i := interest(A, q)
```

By itself:

```text
i -/-> support(q)
i -/-> truth(q)
i -/-> authority(q)
i -/-> later_output
```

Now introduce a real selector occurrence:

```text
r := SELECT(policy=p, basis=i)
```

and let it change the loaded neighborhood:

```text
i ──basis──> r ──selects──> context ──basis──> output
p ──basis──/
```

The interest receipt did not retroactively become a cause.

**The read/selection occurrence entered history.**

This is important because it means the existing causal grammar may already be sufficient. No universal `CONSUMES` edge is required unless executable specimens prove ordinary basis/formation ancestry becomes ambiguous.

## The paired specimen

The strongest executable form is not `ACCESS-ANCESTRY-001` or `PASSED-ROAD-001` alone. It is their collision.

### World

A projection lawfully exposes three possible roads:

```text
A   B   C
```

The actor:

```text
contacts A
contacts C
attends C
uses C in formation
never contacts B
```

Meanwhile C was reachable only because an index/decoder/selector surfaced it.

Preserve separately:

```text
available_set = {A,B,C}
contacted_set = {A,C}
noticed_set   = {C}
causal_parents(output) includes C
```

B remains:

```text
exposed / not-contacted
```

Nothing may infer why B was passed over.

### Then delete the route

Delete the discovery index after the claim has already been found.

Expected:

```text
WHY BELIEVED     survives
WHY USED         survives
WHY REACHABLE    becomes non-reproducible or degraded
```

Rebuild the index from its lawful source model and reachability may become reproducible again without changing the evidentiary ancestry of the already-supported claim.

That is the key result:

> **Discovery infrastructure can die without killing support. Support can survive while the historical route to acquiring it becomes unreproducible.**

## UNDERSTORY as negative formation field

UNDERSTORY may therefore preserve more than what an actor knew.

It can preserve attributable **opportunity topology**:

```text
visible-but-uncontacted
contacted-but-unattended
attended-but-uninteresting
interesting-but-unsupported
supported-but-unused
used-in-formation
```

These are not fictional counterfactuals.

They are roads the actor's historical world actually contained.

That enables a much stronger question than ordinary hindsight:

> **Could this actor lawfully have known differently from the world they actually inhabited?**

The answer depends not merely on what existed globally, but on what was reachable, exposed, contacted, and decodable at that cut.

## The Front Room consequence

The Front Room sequence has quietly become causal:

```text
stable landmark
→ relevant door
→ bounded traversal
→ encounter
→ changed world
```

The useful correction is:

```text
relevant door
    ↑
may itself have ancestry
```

A door may become relevant because of declared interest, a prior receipt, a query, an index, an instrument, a recommendation, a decoder, a human hint, or some other attributable selector.

The door does not prove what lies behind it.

But **why this door opened rather than another** can matter to the causal history of everything that follows.

## Candidate law

> **ACCESS gives an actor a world. Selection explains why that world became reachable. Encounter determines what actually crosses into the actor's history. Evidence determines what may be supported. Formation determines what materially shapes what comes next.**

Short form:

> **THE DOOR HAS ANCESTRY.**

## Hostile controls

1. **Hidden personalization** — context differs but no selector receipt or declared policy explains why. Refuse at the earliest selection/LOADOUT boundary.
2. **Interest laundering** — an interest receipt is treated as evidentiary support. Refuse semantic inflation.
3. **Exposure inflation** — an exposed road is rewritten as contacted or noticed. Refuse invented epistemic history.
4. **Support-route collapse** — deleting the discovery index is treated as deleting the source support. Refuse genealogy collapse.
5. **Passed-road motive invention** — an uncontacted road is assigned a reason for being ignored. Preserve fog.
