# SIMPLICITY IS A QUOTIENT

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Status:** CANDIDATE

## What was noticed

The FAR-SIDE and BINOCULAR work picked up a missing mathematical boundary.

A simple representation is not lawful merely because it is short, elegant, or capable of regenerating one plausible version of the original complexity.

Compression collapses distinctions.

The question is **which distinctions may be collapsed for which future questions**.

Let `T` be a declared family of future probes/tasks and `O` an observation map. A useful candidate relation is:

```text
x ~_T y

iff

for every p in T,
O(P_p(x)) = O(P_p(y))
```

The subscript is load-bearing.

```text
SAME FOR RENDERING
!=
SAME FOR REPLAY
!=
SAME FOR ARCHAEOLOGY
!=
SAME FOR FUTURE REACHABILITY
!=
SAME FOR EVIDENCE
```

The far-side object is therefore not automatically "the simplest description."

A stronger candidate is:

> **the smallest carrier that preserves the distinctions required by the declared future questions.**

## Why regeneration alone is insufficient

Suppose two detailed worlds collapse to one carrier:

```text
W1 -> C
W2 -> C
```

and an expander can regenerate a plausible `Y` from `C`.

That is reconstructional success.

But now suppose a declared future probe gives:

```text
P(W1) = LEFT
P(W2) = RIGHT
```

Then `C` erased a distinction that remained live for the probe.

The expander may simply have chosen one representative from the information fiber.

So the compression has at least three separate survival debts:

```text
RECONSTRUCTIONAL
Can required material regenerate?

DISCRIMINATIVE
Does it keep apart source worlds that matter differently?

DYNAMIC
Does it preserve the consequences of declared future probes?
```

Passing one does not imply the others.

## Residue changes meaning

This gives the recent Gestalt/Binocular residue ledger a harder interpretation.

Residue need not mean:

```text
we failed to simplify enough
```

It may mean:

```text
our current quotient is too coarse
for a still-live question
```

That makes residue actionable:

```text
RESIDUE
  ↓
find the smallest lawful probe
that distinguishes the collapsed states
  ↓
refine the equivalence relation
  ↓
rerun compression
```

The residue is not automatically explanatory evidence. It is a possible counterexample to the current collapse.

## Useful simplicity may be a basis-selection problem

A second scoutpaste connection sharpened the criterion further.

Two compressions may regenerate equally well while one makes meaningful perturbations much more local.

A useful compression may therefore be recognizable by the **family of experiments it makes cheap to ask**.

```text
meaningful perturbation
  ↓
few independent deltas
  ↓
low unrelated blast radius
```

This suggests:

```text
short compression
!=
useful compression
```

A useful basis does not merely make the source smaller. It exposes coordinates in which lawful counterfactuals remain interpretable.
