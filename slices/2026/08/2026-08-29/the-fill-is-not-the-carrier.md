The new crack appeared one step later. A lawfully witnessed filled cell is still not automatically one atomic higher-order carrier. Contracting its interior can erase distinctions needed by later questions.

```text

```

Formal state abstraction supplies a neighboring grammar. Automata minimization similarly merges states only when future behavior cannot distinguish them for the declared recognition target (Yadav et al. 2022, DOI 10.1155/2022/2798898).

These are methodological neighbors, not validation of HOMEGA.

## Proposed hostile specimen — REIFICATION-ERASURE-001

Build a small filled square or cube with internally distinct coherent paths. Define:


Contract the cell into one macrostate and ask whether the contraction preserves each target.


If that split cannot be produced, the distinction may be empty or already captured elsewhere.

## Counterpressure

- `carrier` may already mean an addressable reference to an uncompressed structure; if so, this amendment shrinks.
- A filler may exist without being unique.
- A quotient safe for one observation family may fail after the observation family expands.
- Formal abstraction theory does not establish that physical or symbolic systems literally operate by these rules.
- Flag complexes show that automatic filling can be lawful when it is explicitly part of the model constitution.
