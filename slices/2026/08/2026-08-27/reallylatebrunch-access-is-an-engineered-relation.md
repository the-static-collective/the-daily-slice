
# PEEL 6 — WEATHER DOES THE SAME THING DIFFERENTLY

Weather is not merely pressure waves.

But atmospheric pressure is a field, and weather contains propagating disturbances in pressure, density, temperature, velocity, moisture, and buoyancy.

```text
p(x,y,z,t)
T(x,y,z,t)
ρ(x,y,z,t)
v(x,y,z,t)
q(x,y,z,t)
```

Atmospheric acoustic waves, gravity waves, Rossby waves, and Lamb waves are different propagating modes with different restoring laws.

The useful shared grammar is therefore not “everything is pressure.”

It is:

```text
DISTURBANCE
+
RESTORING LAW
+
MEDIUM / FIELD
+
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS
+
PROPAGATION
```

Lithosphere and atmosphere become comparable without being collapsed.

```text
EARTHQUAKE:
local instability → elastic/stress propagation

WEATHER:
local atmospheric disturbance → pressure/buoyancy/vorticity propagation
```

This is a structural comparison, not one hidden universal mechanism.
