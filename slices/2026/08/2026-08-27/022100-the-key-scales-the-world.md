## Fruit 1 — the glyph is literally an operator

```text

E3 + 1 -> F3
G3 + 0 -> G3

```

So:

```text

  ↓ 022100

```

Three strings move.
Three strings remain anchors.

The open field has the multiset:

```text

```

The glyph turns it into:

```text

```

C and G lose their doubling.
D gains octave doubling.
E disappears.

The glyph does not merely “name a chord.”

It performs a sparse transformation on a receiving field.

## Fruit 5 — local locks, no hidden global master pulse

The doubled D is an exact octave relation in the ideal model:

```text
D4 = 2 × D3
```

Several other partial ladders come very close to simple-ratio coincidence.

But the whole equal-tempered chord does **not** close into one exact finite common period. Its non-octave equal-tempered ratios include irrational powers of `2^(1/12)`.

So the stronger description is:

> **Globally quasi-periodic; locally full of near-locks and one exact octave family.**

This kills a tempting overclaim before it grows legs:

```text
many beautiful local relations
!=
one secret exact master frequency underneath everything
```

## Fruit 6 — frequency differences are not automatically new tones

```text
A2 - C2 ≈ 44.999 Hz
G3 - D3 ≈ 49.612 Hz
```

Those numbers are real arithmetic.

But:

```text
frequency difference
!=
measured spectral component
!=
perceived combination tone
```

Actual beating and combination products depend on partials, nonlinearities, strings, body, pickups, amplification, ear, room, and execution.

The arithmetic is a map of places to inspect.
It is not yet a recording of what happened there.

## Small correction caught before durability

The conversational deep-dive initially summarized one illustrative roughness-model comparison as about `9–14%` rougher than the open tuning.

Before carrying that number forward, the calculation was rerun with the model specified.

A standard Sethares-style pairwise formulation, `1/n` harmonic amplitudes, and 4–12 harmonics per string gives approximately:

```text
8–10.3% greater modeled inter-string roughness
```

for `022100` relative to open `CGCEGD` in that toy spectrum.

Including within-string partial interactions gives roughly:

```text
7.8–9.5%
```

This is **not a perceptual measurement** and not a claim about the physical guitar.

The correction is more useful than the number:

> **Do not preserve a psychoacoustic percentage without preserving the model that generated it.**
