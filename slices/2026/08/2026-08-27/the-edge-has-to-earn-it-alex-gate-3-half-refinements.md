# 1 — THE UNIT OF SKEPTICISM MOVES TO THE EDGE

ALEX already knows how to preserve attributable objects and claims.

The newer event-field work suggests a sharper question:

> **What authorizes this edge?**

A useful candidate field is:

```text
H = (E, Ρ)

E = attributable occurrences
Ρ = typed relation claims
```

A relation claim may eventually need fields such as:

```text
ρ = {
  type,
  from,
  to,
  basis,
  minted_at,
  receipt,
  status,
  authority
}
```

This does not mean ALEX needs a giant universal ontology of relation types.

It means a consequential edge should be able to answer:

```text
WHY DOES THIS EDGE EXIST?
WHEN WAS IT MINTED?
WHAT BASIS SUPPORTS IT?
WHAT AUTHORITY ALLOWED THIS TYPE OF RELATION?
WHAT WOULD FALSIFY OR SEVER IT?
```

An occurrence may simply have happened.

An edge tells a story about how two occurrences belong together.

That is where skepticism should become strongest.

Working law:

```text
NO RELATION SILENTLY MINTS ANOTHER RELATION.
```

Examples:

```text
happened-before   != formation-depends-on
formation         != evidentiary support
attention         != adoption
interest          != evidence
availability      != knowledge
serialization     != causal history
projection equality != historical identity
```

# 2 — CAUSAL-LINE-001 SHOULD PERMIT BRAIDS

The human-facing name `CAUSAL-LINE-001` remains good.

But its internal object should not assume that genuine continuity is one literal chain.

A relation may survive through redundant carriers:

```text
       carrier 1
      /         \
A ── carrier 2 ── B
      \         /
       carrier 3
```

So the candidate continuity object becomes a **causal support graph**.

The hostile remove-one-carrier test therefore deepens:

```text
remove carrier X
      ↓
does relation R still remain attributable?
```

And then:

```text
what is the minimum carrier set
whose removal severs the claimed relation?
```

This introduces a useful distinction among:

```text
SINGLE-THREAD CONTINUITY
REDUNDANT CONTINUITY
BRAIDED CONTINUITY
INFERRED BRIDGE
SEVERED CONTINUITY
```

These are candidate dispositions, not yet ontology.

## The crucial dual-graph split

Do not collapse:

```text
G_causal
what allegedly happened historically
```

with:

```text
G_evidence
why ALEX is justified in believing
that each alleged causal segment happened
```

This creates two different severance questions:

```text
CAUSAL SEVERANCE
What historical carrier set would have to disappear
for relation R actually to break?

EVIDENTIARY SEVERANCE
What evidence set would have to disappear
for ALEX to lose justification for claiming R?
```

Those can diverge sharply.

Example:

```text
three genuinely independent historical carriers
all documented by one surviving manuscript
```

Then:

```text
causal redundancy      = high
independent evidence   = low
```

The receipts do not become the road.

The road does not become justified merely because one receipt narrates it beautifully.

Working refinement:

> **Continuity has topology. Evidence for continuity has a different topology. Preserve both.**

# 3 — DECODER PROVENANCE IS NOT ENOUGH; RECEIPT THE SEARCH

`POSTHOC-KEY-FITTING` originally asked whether the chosen decoder was selected before or after the interesting result became visible.

That remains correct.

But the winning decoder alone understates the real formation process.

Compare:

```text
one frozen decoder
      ↓
unseen carrier
      ↓
surprising result
```

with:

```text
known target
      ↓
8,417 flexible decoders tried
      ↓
one spectacular match selected
```

The final projection may be identical.

The evidentiary situation is not.

ALEX therefore may need a **decoder-search receipt**, not merely a decoder receipt.

Candidate fields:

```text
DECODER-SEARCH-RECEIPT

candidate_family
constraints_before_target
candidate_decoders_considered
search_space_size_or_bound
free_parameters
fitted_parameters
target_known_before_search
result_seen_before_selection
selection_rule
winning_decoder
holdout_material
holdout_result
preregistered_constraints
```

The deeper candidate concept is:

> **interpretive freedom**

Do not force this into one universal scalar yet.

Different decoder families have different kinds of freedom:

```text
10 explicit binary switches
!=
one unconstrained natural-language reinterpretation
```

The important first move is attributable structure, not fake precision.

Working evidentiary principle:

```text
EXPLORATORY VALUE MAY REMAIN HIGH
WHILE CONFIRMATORY WEIGHT REMAINS LOW.
```

Posthoc interpretation is not forbidden.

Cooking the books by forgetting how much freedom preceded the winning interpretation is.

# 4 — TIME + RELATION + LENS BECOME AN EDGE-ADMISSIBILITY TRIAD

Gate 3½ now has a useful symmetry.

For a consequential candidate edge:

```text
A --R--> B
```

ask three independent questions.

## TIME

```text
Could the relevant basis actually have been available
at the historical / observer cut being claimed?
```

## RELATION

```text
What attributable causal or evidentiary structure
actually carries relation R?
```

## LENS

```text
How constrained was the decoder or interpretive procedure
before it produced the interesting reading?
```

Compactly:

```text
                EDGE CANDIDATE
                     │
          ┌──────────┼──────────┐
          ▼          ▼          ▼
        TIME      RELATION      LENS
          │          │          │
          └──────────┼──────────┘
                     ▼
               EDGE DISPOSITION
```

This is not a master ontology.

It is an admission discipline.

ALEX already asks:

> **Where did this claim come from?**

Gate 3½ adds:

> **Could you have known it then?**

> **Is the claimed relationship actually carried?**

> **How much freedom existed to make the lens produce this reading?**

Working compression:

```text
TIME + RELATION + LENS
```

or:

```text
WHEN COULD YOU KNOW IT?
HOW IS IT CONNECTED?
WHY DID YOU READ IT THAT WAY?
```

But absence needs typing too.

Candidate `absence_reason` vocabulary:

```text
UNAVAILABLE
outside lawful cut

UNVISITED
available, but traversal never crossed it

OMITTED
encountered, deliberately excluded

NOTICED_NOT_USED
encountered, but not formation basis

SEARCHED_NOT_FOUND

```

These are materially different worlds of reasoning.

Especially:

```text
SEARCHED_NOT_FOUND != ABSENT
```

and:

```text
EXISTED SOMEWHERE != AVAILABLE HERE
```

This lets ALEX distinguish:

> You ignored evidence.

from:

> That evidence existed somewhere but was outside this traversal's lawful field of view.

That distinction is not cosmetic.

It is historical accountability.

# 6 — FORMATION-KNOCKOUT-001

The partial-order work exposed another crucial distinction:

```text
B1 ≺ A2
```

may lawfully mean:

> B1 was in the attributable causal past of A2.

It does **not** automatically mean:

```text
FORMATION_DEPENDS_ON(A2, B1)
```

and neither means:

```text
SUPPORTS(B1, claim_in_A2)
```

That suggests an executable hostile specimen using matched observer cuts.

## Candidate experiment — `FORMATION-KNOCKOUT-001`

Construct two bounded worlds:

```text
WORLD α
candidate basis B is lawfully available
before formation event A2

WORLD β
same relevant cut and constitution
except B is lawfully unavailable
```

Hold everything else as fixed as the system permits.

Then compare:

```text
A2α
A2β
```

Questions:

```text
Did removing B alter the resulting formation?
Did the output remain invariant?
Did only presentation change?
Did another basis substitute for B?
Was B necessary, sufficient, neither, or unresolved?
```

Interpretation discipline:

```text
with B    -> X
without B -> X
```

may count as evidence against B being **necessary** formation basis in that bounded specimen.

But:

```text
with B    -> X
without B -> Y
```

still does not prove:

```text
B caused X
```

It establishes a dependency worth further pressure.

This is the causal remove-one test turned onto **epistemic formation**.

3rdi is unusually well positioned to construct the matched cuts.

ALEX is unusually well positioned to judge what relation the result actually earns.

# 7 — FORMATION ROLES STAY SMALL

The Osiris / later-clue problem remains a keeper.

A later clue may explain or reveal the category of an earlier idea without causing that earlier idea to exist.

Initial vocabulary should remain deliberately restrained:

```text
caused_by
enabled_by
revealed_by
named_by
falsified_by
strengthened_by
```

Do not build a large relation taxonomy because it is aesthetically satisfying.

Add types only when materially different specimens require them.

`compressed_by` can initially remain an annotation/subtype of `revealed_by`.

`redirected_by` may be better represented as a later causal consequence rather than a primitive historical relation.

## `RETROACTIVE-ORIGIN`

Keep the hostile specimen simple:

```text
Idea A documented at t1.
Clue B encountered at t2.

Later narrative:
"B caused A."

where t2 > t1.
```

Refuse that origin claim.

Possible lawful replacements include:

```text
B revealed A's category
B named A
B strengthened an interpretation of A
B independently resembled A
```

Relevance is not causation.

A later decoder cannot become an earlier cause merely because it fits beautifully.

# 10 — THE DEEPER QUESTION

The four refinements converge on one question:

> **What would have to be different for this edge not to exist?**

That decomposes into:

```text
WHAT AUTHORIZES THIS EDGE?

WHAT WOULD BREAK THIS EDGE?

WHAT FREEDOM EXISTED TO INVENT THIS EDGE?
```

That is stronger than provenance alone.

ALEX no longer asks only whether a claim has ancestry.

It asks whether the **relation itself** survives hostile removal of the things allegedly carrying it.

It asks whether the observer could lawfully have reached the evidence at the relevant cut.

It asks whether the lens was constrained before the interesting result appeared.

It asks whether later understanding is being smuggled backward as earlier causation.

It asks whether attention, availability, formation, support, chronology, and serialization are being silently collapsed into stronger relations.

The candidate research machine therefore becomes:

```text
OCCURRENCES
    +
TYPED RELATION CLAIMS
    +
LOCAL TRANSITIONS
    +
OBSERVER CUTS
    +
DECODER SEARCH RECEIPTS
    +
HOSTILE EDGE REMOVAL
```

while preserving:

```text
NO RELATION SILENTLY MINTS ANOTHER RELATION.
```
