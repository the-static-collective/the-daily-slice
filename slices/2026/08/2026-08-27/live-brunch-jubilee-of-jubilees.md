## 2. The Book of Jubilees actually scales the operator upward

This is not merely a modern phrase.

The Second Temple **Book of Jubilees** divides history into **49-year jubilee periods**. Jubilees 50:4 says that from Adam to Moses's present there have been **49 jubilees plus one week and two years**, with **40 years still remaining** until Israel crosses into Canaan.

Arithmetic:

```text
49 × 49 = 2401
2401 + 7 + 2 = 2410
2410 + 40 = 2450
2450 = 50 × 49
```

Scholarly summaries identify the macro-logic explicitly:

```text
ordinary Jubilee:
enslaved person released
ancestral land returned

macro-Jubilee:
Israel released from Egypt
Israel returned to ancestral land
```

So the **fiftieth jubilee period** plays the role that the fiftieth year plays at the smaller scale.

This is a genuine recursive structure:

```text
JUBILEE : 49 YEARS
::
JUBILEE-OF-JUBILEES : 49 JUBILEE PERIODS
```

Or mathematically:

```text
49 completed jubilee periods
= 49² years
= 2401
= 7⁴

completion of the 50th jubilee period
= 50 × 49
= 2450 years
```

The recursion preserves **release + return** while changing carrier from household/landholding to national history.
