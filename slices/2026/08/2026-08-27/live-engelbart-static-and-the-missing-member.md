## Keeper 001 — Engelbart's 1962 regenerative loop is stronger than the summary implied

Primary witness: Douglas Engelbart, *Augmenting Human Intellect: A Conceptual Framework* (SRI, October 1962).

Engelbart explicitly recommends that augmentation researchers become the **first users of the augmentation means they develop**. His reason is regenerative: successful research outputs should feed directly back into the means by which the researchers perform the research. He describes the team as developing better tools for a class to which **they themselves belong**.

The report then proposes a small group whose assignment is effectively:

> develop means that make you more effective at doing the job of developing those means.

The output is fed back as improved tools and techniques for the next research cycle.

This is not a retrospective reading of Engelbart. In the 1962 report the relevant sections are literally titled **BASIC REGENERATIVE FEATURE** and **TOOLS DEVELOPED AND TOOLS USED**.

Source: https://dougengelbart.org/pubs/augment-3906.html

### What survives contact with the ancient witnesses

The **missing-member** structure is ancient in the Greek-language Osiris narratives we can verify. In Plutarch (*De Iside et Osiride* 18), Isis recovers Osiris's dismembered body except the male member, which was lost in the Nile; she makes a **replica** and consecrates the phallus. Diodorus likewise reports an **image** of the missing member made and installed for cult.

Sources:
- Plutarch / Loeb transcription: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Plutarch%27s_Moralia_(Loeb)/Isis_and_Osiris
- Modern scholarly discussion of `eidolon` / cult image including Diodorus 1.22.7: https://doi.org/10.1163/15700631-bja10098

### GOLD boundary

The commonly repeated **golden phallus** wording has **not yet been established in the oldest witnesses inspected in this run**. Plutarch says replica; Diodorus says image. ## Native Egyptian counterpressure already visible

The Pyramid Texts preserve a much older Osirian generative scene in which Isis comes to Osiris, his seed enters her, and Horus comes forth; nearby text says Horus collects and puts together Osiris's limbs. This means the popular modern composite story must be handled carefully: **native Egyptian generative resurrection imagery and Plutarch's later story of the lost phallus / cult replica are not automatically one single original narrative.**

Example locus: Pyramid Texts Utterance 366–367 (§632–635 in older numbering / translation traditions).
