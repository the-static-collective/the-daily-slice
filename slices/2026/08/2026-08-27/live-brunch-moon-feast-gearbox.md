# 8. Harvest Moon / Sukkot is a stronger physical continuity than “blood moon prophecy”

Sukkot is not merely on a full moon.

It is the full-moon feast held in the autumn-harvest season.

The **Harvest Moon** is the full moon nearest the autumnal equinox. At mid-to-high northern latitudes, the Moon’s path meets the horizon at a shallow angle around this season, producing unusually small delays between successive evening moonrises and historically extending usable evening light during harvest.

So Sukkot sits in a physically distinctive recurring light regime even in years with **no eclipse**.

The 2015 event was therefore:

```text
ordinary recurring Sukkot / harvest-phase architecture
+
rarer node alignment
+
rarer perigee alignment
```

This hierarchy matters.

**The feast’s harvest/full-moon relation is the floor. The eclipse is an occasional overlay.**
