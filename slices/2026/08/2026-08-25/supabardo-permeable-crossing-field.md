# SupaBardo — Permeable Crossing Field

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Status:** CANDIDATE

## What was noticed

SupaBardo became more interesting when treated not as a Human-Witness-only signing adapter and not as one global Supabase bus, but as a possible **portable Crossing Field protocol with local runtimes**.

The visual shorthand is:

> **Permeable membrane, not central nervous system.**

and the harder law beneath it is:

> **Crossings travel. Authority does not.**

Human Witness is currently the first owner/specimen. It already defines SupaBardo as a bounded Supabase-backed field for the interval between encounter and durable consequence, under the law **“Store the crossing, not the canon.”** That project-owned design explicitly refuses to let Presence, Broadcast, RLS, queues, rows, server acknowledgements, or exports silently become identity, assent, legal validity, authority, Corpus OS admission, or universal history.

The new candidate is narrower than “put SupaBardo everywhere.”

It is:

```text
WORLD A
  local meaning
      ↓
LOCAL CROSSING RUNTIME
  bounded relation
  unresolved obligations
  transient coordination
      ↓
TYPED CROSSING EXIT
  says exactly what crossed
  says exactly what did not
      ↓
WORLD B
  local gate
  local meaning
  local consequence or refusal
```

The field may be network-permeable while consequence remains locally constituted.

## The network shape

Candidate participating worlds include:

- Human Witness — exact subject binding and local human-act semantics;
- Groove Rooms — contribution and handoff crossings;
- Upper Room — presence, reading, encounter, formation traces;
- Toaster / ELF — proposals awaiting human admission or refusal;
- Corpus OS — downstream admission/refusal under its own law;
- TranchNode / Git — durable carrier and history;
- Vault — evidence/artifact retention;
- GitBook — projection/navigation, never automatic canon.

The important extraction is not a universal Human Witness receipt.

A portable network carrier should be smaller, closer to a `CrossingExit` / `ExitBundle`, and able to declare:

```text
what crossed
+ subject / crossing binding
+ attributable occurrence refs
+ unresolved obligations
+ terminal condition, if any
+ retention / decay posture
+ return route
+ explicit exclusions
```

Typical exclusions should include, unless independently constituted:

```text
authority
authenticated identity
legal capacity
comprehension
assent inferred from presence
destination consequence
```

Each project may then seal, interpret, admit, refuse, or carry that exit according to its own law.

## State law

A useful correction emerged from the visual pass:

`ENTER · FORM · WITNESS · WAIT · EXIT · DECAY` are better treated as **occurrence classes / ceremony verbs**, not as one mandatory linear finite-state machine.

Formation and witnessing may interleave. Waiting may recur. Multiple obligations may resolve independently.

The smaller candidate lifecycle is:

```text
OPEN
= begun + attributable + unresolved

RESOLVED
= attributable terminal occurrence exists

EXPIRED
= time produced a terminal occurrence
  without synthetic success

ABANDONED
= explicitly closed
  without pretending completion
```

Candidate occurrence classes remain richer:

`ENTER` · `FORM` · `WITNESS` · `OBLIGATION_OPEN` · `WAIT` · `ACT` · `EXPORT` · `DECAY`

## Invariant wall

The visual preserved ten boundaries worth carrying into any future specimen:

```text
OPEN                     ≠ admitted
Presence                  ≠ identity / attention / comprehension / assent
permission                ≠ authority
replay                    ≠ new occurrence
server acknowledgement    ≠ human act
subject digest            ≠ silently mutable mid-crossing
resolution                requires attributable terminal cause
decay                     must not destroy the only required carrier
export success            ≠ destination admission
every destination         performs its own local gate
```

This is the core reason a shared crossing protocol might remain safe: the protocol carries **typed relation and evidence**, not ambient consequence.

## Three extraction shapes considered

### A — One global Supabase bus

**Verdict: reject.**

It would make discovery and reuse easy, but creates semantic gravity, a large blast radius, privacy pressure, and a constant temptation for transport state to become de facto canon.

### B — Copy the adapter everywhere

**Verdict: temporarily tolerable.**

It preserves locality, but the lifecycle semantics will fork and drift if every project invents its own version.

### C — Portable protocol + local membranes

**Verdict: recommended candidate.**

Keep the reusable semantic contract small. Let each project own its local adapter/runtime. Supabase may host a field without becoming the center of authority.

## Promotion test

This slice does **not** justify a SupaBardo repository yet.

The current project-owned promotion ladder already says extraction should wait until a second, meaningfully different crossing kind reuses the same semantics without special-case architecture.

The sharper candidate sequence is:

```text
SB-001
Human signature crossing
inside Human Witness
        ↓
MADDCL0WN / adversarial survival
        ↓
second foreign specimen
        ↓
same portable law survives?
        ↓
only then consider extraction
```

The preferred second proof is **Toaster proposal → human admit / refuse**.

That specimen deliberately strips away signature and legal semantics. If the following shape still survives:

```text
OPEN
  → obligation
  → attributable terminal occurrence
  → typed carrier
  → destination-local admission / refusal
```

then SupaBardo begins to prove that it is a network-level boundary rather than merely an elegant Human Witness adapter.

One success proves feasibility. Two distinct successes begin to prove a reusable boundary.

## Why it matters

This gives the Static Collective a possible answer to a recurring architectural pressure:

> How can worlds remain permeable to one another without letting transit erase jurisdiction?

The answer may be to make the **crossing itself** portable while refusing to make its **meaning** globally portable.

That would let Human Witness, Groove Rooms, Upper Room, Toaster/ELF, Storyship-like crossings, Corpus OS, durable carriers, and future worlds meet through bounded relations without collapsing into one universal database, event bus, authority graph, or canonical state machine.

The line worth carrying is:

> **A crossing may be shared. Its meaning is not.**

## Epistemic split

**Documented fact:** Human Witness currently owns the accepted SupaBardo architecture seed; that spec defines SupaBardo as a bounded crossing-field specimen, explicitly rejects a standalone repo for the first proof, and requires a second meaningfully different crossing before extraction is considered.  
**Inference:** The repeated distinctions around open crossing, typed carrier, local gates, and non-inherited authority suggest that a smaller portable crossing protocol may be reusable across worlds while project-local semantics remain local.  
**Speculation:** SupaBardo may eventually deserve its own repository or service as a network primitive. That claim is intentionally unproved.
