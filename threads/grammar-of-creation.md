**Thread status:** OPEN · NON-CANONICAL · RESEARCH CONTINUITY  
**Thread rule:** this page links formation witnesses and testable descendants. It does not declare that poetic recurrence, mathematical structure, musical structure, model output, or metaphysical interpretation are equivalent or causally unified.

## Why this thread exists

A recurring Static Collective question has become specific enough to preserve across slices:

> **What is the smallest grammar by which retained difference can produce further lawful structure?**

The current candidate is not a symbol dictionary. It is a transformation grammar.

```text
THING / CARRIER
      |
      | declared operator
      v
PROJECTION
      |
      | compare lawful paths
      v
DELTA
      |
      | retain without premature collapse
      v
possible new carrier
```

The working requirement is strict:

```text
carrier != projection
relation != identity
recurrence != authority
similarity != cause
operator must travel with the receipt
```

## Candidate vocabulary

- **CARRIER** — retained input presented to an operator.
- **OPERATOR** — declared transformation / decoder / action.
- **PROJECTION** — typed result under that operator.
- **ORDER** — sequence of composed transformations.
- **DELTA** — attributable difference between lawful paths.
- **RECURRENCE** — later re-entry of a carrier, projection, or delta.
- **NON-COLLAPSE** — preservation of live distinctions rather than premature equivalence.
- **APERTURE** — candidate pre-semantic cut that retains signal, attributable context, and unresolved gaps before semantic-role classification.
- **SENSE FIELD** — bounded set of candidate readings retained in unresolved tension under one declared cut; availability does not establish human intent.
- **CREATION** — reserved as a research term for the possible production of further structured possibility from retained difference; not yet a formal primitive.
- **RESOLVER** — candidate term for a relation that makes a fact / target addressable through a name, trace, representation, provenance edge, or other declared identifier; does not itself establish causal reach.
- **RELATION PROMOTION** — candidate term for a proposed operation by which a relation on a representation / carrier is claimed to become a relation on its referent; requires domain-specific admission and is not assumed lawful.

## Promotion gates

Before this thread earns stronger language, require at least:

1. **Formal specimen:** a typed Dogram representation of carrier/operator/projection/path/delta.
2. **Counterexample:** a case where order changes but the delta is structureless or useless.
3. **Commuting control:** a case where operator order does not matter and Delta vanishes.
4. **Cross-domain control:** at least one musical or linguistic example whose structure is independently specified rather than numerologically retrofitted.
5. **Observer receipt:** distinguish which decoder/operator was available to which observer and when.
6. **No silent metaphysics:** mathematical beauty, recurrence, or autobiographical resonance cannot promote causal or theological interpretation by itself.
7. **Name hostile controls:** test alias, homonym, translation, renaming, anonymous reference, forged authority, and correct-name/wrong-relation cases before treating name as provenance rather than mere addressability.
8. **Relation-promotion kill test:** exhibit at least one domain where a representation is correctly resolved to a fact but a proposed relation demonstrably does **not** propagate to the referent.
