# Proximity / Continuity / Logos

A durable pointer thread for slices that examine relation to source, rupture and return, continuity through incarnation/re-entry, and the Logos-as-ordering-principle analogy.

This thread preserves recurrence. It does not make theological interpretations, architectural analogies, biological hypotheses, or linked project proposals canonical merely because they appear together.

## Working questions

- Is death usefully understood, in part, as the terminal trajectory of rupture from the source of life?
- Is repentance structurally a reversal of relational direction after rupture?
- Does kingdom language repeatedly demote ordinary rank beneath proximity to the King?
- Why does Christian redemption preserve Adamic human continuity rather than replace humanity with an unrelated creation?
- What does the Logos becoming flesh reveal about repair occurring from inside a damaged continuity?
- When is "compile into the runtime" a clarifying analogy, and where does the analogy break?
- Can a designed world such as Derek Derrick Dark require a resident to reconstruct the world's ordering relation from within rather than receive an external solution?
- Is contrast itself good while the catastrophe begins when an evaluative axis becomes the center?
- Can a tiny alteration in a recursive carrier produce world-scale consequences through gene-expression, development, culture, and relational feedback without requiring the alteration itself to grow?
- Does the biblical lineage preserve material purity, or something closer to attributable / covenantal continuity through imperfect carriers?
- Can "distributed mystery" and seed language illuminate the compile-from-within analogy without pretending Scripture describes software or hidden biological code?
