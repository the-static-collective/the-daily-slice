What becomes network-permeable

  • crossing reference + exact subject binding
  • participant relations + bounded capabilities
  • typed unresolved obligations
  • occurrence references + terminal condition
  • retention / decay policy + return route
  • vendor-neutral ExitBundle

What stays local

  • legal / human meaning of SIGN, ASSENT, REFUSE
  • identity and capacity judgments
  • trust admission and Action Warrants
  • canonical world-state
  • consequential interpretation
  • any assumption that crossing grants authority
Do not export a universal Human Witness receipt format as the network protocol. Export the smaller crossing carrier. Human Witness, Toaster, Groove Rooms, Storyship, and future worlds each interpret or seal that carrier under their own local law.

Ceremony verbs are not one mandatory linear FSM

ENTER · FORM · WITNESS · WAIT · EXIT · DECAY describe occurrences. FORM and WITNESS may interleave, WAIT may recur, and multiple obligations may resolve independently.

OPENBegun + attributable + unresolved.
RESOLVEDAn attributable terminal occurrence exists.
EXPIREDTime produced a terminal occurrence without synthetic success.
ABANDONEDExplicitly closed without pretending completion.

Invariant wall

OPEN ≠ admitted.
Presence ≠ identity, attention, comprehension, or assent.
Permission ≠ authority.
Replay ≠ new occurrence.
Server acknowledgement ≠ human act.
Subject digest may not silently mutate mid-crossing.
Resolution requires an attributable terminal cause.
Decay must not destroy the only required carrier.
Export success ≠ destination admission.
Every destination performs its own local gate.
Typed carrier rule: the exit declares what crossed—lineage, evidence references, obligations, terminal occurrence, subject binding—and explicitly what did not cross by default: authority, authenticated identity, legal capacity, comprehension, or destination consequence.

Preferred second proof

Toaster proposal → human admit / refuse. It deliberately removes signature/legal semantics. If OPEN → obligation → terminal occurrence → typed carrier → local admission still works, SupaBardo begins to prove itself as a network-level boundary rather than an elegant Human Witness adapter.

A crossing may be shared. Its meaning is not. Nodes may meet in the same bounded field, but the field only carries attributable relations and typed evidence. Every world decides locally what, if anything, arrival changes.
